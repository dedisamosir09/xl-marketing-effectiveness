Advanced Model DetailsMethodology, validation, uncertainty, and production readiness
Primary model

Google Meridian Bayesian MMM provides contribution, response curves, carryover, uncertainty, and scenario planning.

Challenger check

Ridge regression is used as a stability and directional hypothesis check—not as a second source of truth in the UI.

Calibration path

Production deployment should incorporate experiment priors, business controls, holdouts, and recurring model refreshes.

POC boundary: the current model window is 144 days. Budget and hiatus outputs remain directional until the model is calibrated with production controls and experiments.
Interpretation guardrail MMM estimates incremental impact at channel × time grain after accounting for baseline demand and modeled controls. Results are not platform attribution and should not be filtered to campaign, placement, or customer-journey level.
